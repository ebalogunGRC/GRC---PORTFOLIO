# Project 01 — NovaCare Enterprise Risk Assessment & Risk Register

**Area:** Risk Management  
**Primary methodology:** NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1  
**Supporting references:** NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5; ISO/IEC 27001:2022

## Scenario
NovaCare is a fictional healthcare technology organization operating cloud services, identity infrastructure, endpoints, public web applications and third-party integrations. This case study assesses 25 modeled cybersecurity risk scenarios.

## Scope and approach
1. Identify critical business and technology assets and associated threats.
2. Describe vulnerabilities and existing control assumptions.
3. Score inherent likelihood and impact on a 1–5 scale.
4. Prioritize risks for management attention.
5. Propose controls and risk treatments, with owners and expected evidence.
6. Model potential residual risk following treatment.
7. Present the results through a clickable heat map and filterable risk register.

## Executive summary
- **25** simulated risk scenarios.
- **7 Critical**, **17 High**, **1 Medium** inherent priority risks.
- **9** risk domains.
- **48% modeled reduction** in aggregate risk points in the original workbook scenario. This is a forecast, not a validated outcome.

## Key recommendations
- Apply phishing-resistant MFA, least privilege, privileged account monitoring and recurring access reviews.
- Prioritize patching and continuous vulnerability visibility for internet-facing systems.
- Centralize logs and test incident escalation procedures.
- Improve vendor risk reviews, backup integrity testing and disaster recovery exercises.

## Evidence expectations
IAM exports, access review sign-offs, vulnerability scan outputs, approved policy documents, vendor assessment records, backup restoration test results and remediation tickets. These are **expected evidence types**, not actual evidence collected from a real organization.
