Project 08 · AI governance

AI Governance & Use-Case Risk Review

An inventory and risk review of 10 AI use cases at NovaCare, from a patient-facing symptom checker to unapproved public chatbots. Each use case is tiered, assessed against the four NIST AI RMF functions, and given a decision by the AI Review Board.

NIST AI RMF 1.0NIST AI 600-1 (GenAI profile)ISO/IEC 42001:2023HIPAA Privacy & Security Rules
OrganizationNovaCare Health Technologies (fictional)
PeriodAug – Oct 2026; status as of Oct 31, 2026
Prepared byEmmanuel Balogun

Engagement summary

Objective

Give leadership a complete view of where AI is used and a consistent way to approve, condition or stop it.

Scope

10 use cases found through a staff survey, procurement records and proxy logs, including shadow AI.

Approach

Use-case intake form, data-flow review, vendor contract review, and gap assessment against AI RMF Govern, Map, Measure and Manage.

Criteria

High tier: affects clinical, hiring or financial decisions about people, or handles PHI with limited human review.

10AI use cases inventoried
4High-risk use cases
3paused, prohibited or on hold
6use cases with untested accuracy, bias or drift

Conclusion and key findings

Overall rating
Needs improvement

Low-risk internal uses are well governed. The program now needs testing evidence for High-risk use cases and a policy that stops PHI from reaching public AI tools.

AI-01

Patient symptom checker paused

It gave care-level advice with no clinical validation and no red-flag escalation. Restart requires a validation study and CMIO sign-off.

AI-09

340 staff used 11 unapproved AI services in one month

No AI acceptable-use policy exists. An approved enterprise assistant and a block for PHI users are the fix (R-21).

AI-05

Resume screening on hold pending a bias audit

The tool decides who gets an interview and has never been tested for adverse impact.

AI-04

Transcription approved with contract conditions

Model-training rights removed, US-only processing required, and medication-name accuracy to be measured on 200 notes.

Method and criteria

How the assessment was performed

  1. Build the inventory from a survey, procurement records and network logs.
  2. Complete an intake form for each use case: purpose, data, decisions affected, human oversight.
  3. Assign a risk tier using the criteria table.
  4. Assess gaps against AI RMF Govern, Map, Measure and Manage.
  5. Present to the AI Review Board for a decision and conditions.
  6. Re-review High-tier use cases every 6 months and after model changes.
