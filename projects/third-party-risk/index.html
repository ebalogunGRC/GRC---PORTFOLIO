Key findings

The EHR partner's SOC 2 opinion is qualified

Shared admin accounts at Clinilink, plus an any-to-any VPN tunnel into NovaCare's hub. Approved only with a corrective action plan and network restriction.

TPR-06

PHI is in a support tool with no BAA

Agents attach patient documents in Helpdesko. Renewal is blocked until a BAA is signed and retention is limited.

The AI transcription contract allowed model training

An amendment to prohibit training and require US-only processing is a condition of approval (see AI-04).

Contractor technicians share accounts

Two generic remote-support accounts with no session recording. Access suspended until named accounts with MFA are issued.

Scope and approach

Objective

Establish a repeatable tiering and review process and clear the backlog of unreviewed critical vendors.

Scope

12 vendors: all Tier 1 vendors, every vendor with PHI access, and a sample of lower tiers.

Approach

Inherent-risk questionnaire for tiering, review of SOC reports (opinion, exceptions, CUECs, bridge letters), contract and BAA checks.

Criteria

Tier 1 reassessed annually, Tier 2 every 2 years, Tier 3–4 at renewal. PHI access requires a signed BAA.

Method and criteria

How the assessment was performed

  1. Classify each vendor with the inherent-risk questionnaire to set its tier.
  2. Collect assurance: SOC 2 Type II, ISO certificates, SIG responses, pen-test summaries.
  3. Review SOC reports for opinion, exceptions, period coverage, bridge letters and CUECs.
  4. Check contracts for BAA, breach notice, audit rights and data location terms.
  5. Rate residual risk and record a decision with conditions and an owner.
  6. Schedule the next review by tier and track conditions to closure.
