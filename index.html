Method

How I work

The same discipline runs through every assessment, whatever the framework.

  1. 1 · Scope

    Agree what matters

    Define systems, data and criteria with the people who own them.

  2. 2 · Evidence

    Test, don't ask

    Inspect configurations, logs and samples rather than relying on interviews alone.

  3. 3 · Rate

    Score consistently

    Use published scales so every rating can be explained and repeated.

  4. 4 · Recommend

    Make it actionable

    Specific fixes with an owner, a due date and a link to the risk they reduce.

  5. 5 · Verify

    Close with proof

    Retest before closing, and record formal acceptance when risk stays above appetite.

Condition

What I found

“3 of 25 leavers stayed enabled 6 to 14 business days after termination.”
