# Emmanuel Balogun — Interactive GRC Portfolio

Live site: https://ebalogungrc.github.io/GRC---PORTFOLIO/

## About

A professional, responsive portfolio showcasing practical, interactive Governance, Risk & Compliance case studies. Emmanuel's background is in IT support; the work demonstrates the transition into GRC, IT Risk, Compliance, and IT Audit.

## Featured projects

1. **NovaCare Enterprise Risk Assessment & Risk Register** — a simulated 25-risk assessment with interactive risk heat map, risk register, inherent/residual scoring, treatments, and control mappings. Frameworks: NIST SP 800-30, NIST SP 800-53, ISO/IEC 27001.
2. **NovaCare NIST CSF 2.0 Gap Assessment & Control Mapping** — an assessment of 36 selected CSF outcomes with maturity dashboard, current state, evidence requirements, remediation and control mappings.

NovaCare is a **fictional** healthcare technology organization. All data, results, evidence examples, and risk reductions are **simulated**, not findings from a real audit or client engagement. The 36-outcome sample is not a full NIST CSF assessment.
