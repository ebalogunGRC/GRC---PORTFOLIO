Key findings

BIA-02

The EHR hub's last recovery took longer than clinics can tolerate

31 hours, against a 4-hour RTO and an 8-hour MTD set by the CTO and CMIO. Manual queue rebuild was the bottleneck.

BIA-03

Claims processing has never been restored

Its backups share the production account with no object lock, so ransomware could take both (see R-02).

BIA-08

No fallback when security monitoring is down

If the SIEM is unavailable there is no agreed way to keep watching for attacks.

BIA-01, BIA-04

Portal and identity recover within target

Both met RTO in 2026 exercises, which shows the testing approach works and can be extended.

Scope and approach

Objective

Agree recovery targets with the business and find where technical recovery capability falls short.

Scope

10 business services, from the patient portal (Tier 1) to the corporate website (Tier 4).

Approach

BIA interviews with business owners, dependency mapping, review of timed restore and failover exercise records.

Criteria

Tested RTO must be within target; target RTO must sit well inside MTD. Tier 1 services tested quarterly.

Method and criteria

How the assessment was performed

  1. Interview business owners to describe impact over time if each service stops.
  2. Set MTD from the point impact becomes unacceptable; derive RTO and RPO inside it.
  3. Map technology and vendor dependencies for each service.
  4. Collect results of the most recent timed recovery for each service.
  5. Compare tested results with targets and record gaps with owners.
  6. Schedule tests by tier and feed gaps into the risk register.
