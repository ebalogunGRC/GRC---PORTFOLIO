Executive assessment

Identity, cloud data access, application security and monitoring drive the highest modeled exposure. Assign owners, approve risk treatments and track evidence-backed closure. A risk score alone does not establish control effectiveness.

Risk acceptance criteria

Critical (20–25): immediate treatment priority. High (12–19): timely action. Medium (6–11): tracked treatment plan. Low (1–5): maintain and monitor controls.

GRC capabilities demonstrated

Risk identification and analysis · inherent and residual scoring · threat and vulnerability analysis · risk treatment · control mapping · evidence planning · remediation ownership · executive reporting · risk monitoring.

Frameworks & limitations

NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1, NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 are used as methodological references. NovaCare is fictional; data, scores, treatments and trends are simulated. No real client evidence or audit conclusions are claimed.
