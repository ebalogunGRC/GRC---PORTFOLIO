Key findings

ITGC-02

Privileged access reviews skip AWS entirely

Reperforming the Q2 review found 4 engineers who had changed roles but kept AdministratorAccess in production. Management removed them on Sep 12.

ITGC-09

Tier 1 restore tests were not performed

No tests in Q1 or Q2. The Q3 EHR hub restore took 31 hours against a 4-hour RTO.

ITGC-01, ITGC-12

Account lifecycle depends on manual steps

12% of sampled leavers stayed active for 6–14 days, and 27% of service accounts have no owner.

ITGC-05

Admins could bypass deployment controls

2 of 25 deployments had no change ticket. The bypass was removed during fieldwork.

ITGC-01Leaver accounts are disabled within 1 business day of the HR termination date.
Access management · Per event		25 of 483DeficiencyIAM Manager
Test procedure

Matched HR termination report to Entra ID disable timestamps for a random sample.

Condition

3 of 25 leavers (12%) stayed enabled 6, 9 and 14 business days after termination.

Criteria

Access Management Standard §4.2: disable within 1 business day.

Cause

Terminations entered in HR after the effective date do not trigger the IT ticket.

Effect

Former staff could reach email and SaaS apps containing PHI.

Recommendation

Trigger deprovisioning from the HR system via SCIM and reconcile leavers weekly.

Management response

Agreed. SCIM provisioning from Workday to Entra ID planned for January 2027.

ITGC-02Privileged access to production is reviewed and recertified each quarter.
Access management · Quarterly		2 of 22Significant deficiencyIAM Manager

Agreed. Accounts removed on 2026-09-12; scope expanded for the Q4 review.

ITGC-04Passwords and MFA settings meet the authentication standard.
Access management · Continuous		1 of 11DeficiencyIT Operations Manager
Test procedure

Inspected Entra password policy, Conditional Access policies and MFA registration report.

Condition

MFA is enforced for all users, but legacy authentication is allowed for 3 shared mailboxes, which bypasses MFA.

Criteria

Authentication Standard §2.3: legacy protocols blocked.

Cause

Exclusion created in 2024 for a scanner-to-email workflow and never removed.

Effect

Replace the scan-to-email workflow with an app registration and remove the exclusion.

Agreed. Target end of November 2026.

ITGC-05Production changes are approved before deployment.
Change management · Many per day		25 of 11242DeficiencyEngineering Director
Test procedure

Pulled deployment logs from GitHub Actions and matched each deployment to an approved change ticket.

Condition

2 of 25 deployments (8%) had no change ticket. Both were emergency fixes deployed by a repository admin.

Criteria

Change Management Policy §3: every production change approved; emergency changes approved within 2 business days.

Cause

Admins can bypass branch protection, and the emergency-change process is not followed after hours.

Effect

Untested or malicious code could reach production without review.

Recommendation

Remove admin bypass; require retrospective approval of emergency changes within 2 business days.

Management response

Agreed. Bypass removed 2026-10-20; emergency process retraining scheduled.

Method and criteria

How the assessment was performed

  1. Walk through each control with its owner to confirm design.
  2. Obtain a complete, system-generated population and confirm its completeness.
  3. Select a random sample sized to the control frequency.
  4. Inspect or reperform each sample item and document exceptions.
  5. Write findings in condition, criteria, cause, effect form and agree management responses.
  6. Retest remediated controls before closing findings.
