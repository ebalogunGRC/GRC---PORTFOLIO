Executive findings & management monitoring

The simulated risk register highlights priority exposures that require accountable treatment plans, measurable outcomes, and governance oversight.

01

Privileged identity exposure

Privileged accounts and cloud access are critical control areas. Prioritize phishing-resistant MFA, access certification and privileged activity logging.

Accountable owner: IAM / Security
02

Detection and response

Incomplete event monitoring and untested escalation pathways can delay detection and containment. Strengthen log coverage and exercise response playbooks.

Accountable owner: SOC / IR
03

Cloud and supplier dependencies

Patient-data access, misconfigurations and critical suppliers require continuous assurance, documented controls and evidence-backed treatment decisions.

Accountable owner: Cloud / Vendor Risk
