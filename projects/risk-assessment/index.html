Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

A FY2026 enterprise risk assessment for NovaCare Health Technologies: 25 cybersecurity risks scored on a 5×5 likelihood-and-impact scale, each with a treatment decision, accountable owner, control mapping and target residual score. It also serves as the company's HIPAA risk analysis of record.

NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5ISO/IEC 27001:2022HIPAA §164.308(a)(1)
OrganizationNovaCare Health Technologies (fictional)
PeriodFeb – Apr 2026; status as of Oct 31, 2026
Prepared byEmmanuel Balogun

Engagement summary

Objective

Identify and prioritize the cybersecurity risks that could disrupt patient services or expose PHI, and agree treatment with owners.

Scope

All production systems, the corporate Microsoft 365 tenant, endpoints, and the 14 vendors with PHI access.

Approach

Asset and threat workshops with 9 risk owners, review of prior audit and pen-test results, scoring per NIST SP 800-30 Appendix G–I.

Criteria

Risk appetite: target residual ≤ 9 (Moderate). Any High residual needs written acceptance by an executive.

25risks assessed across 10 domains
4rated Critical before treatment
52%reduction in aggregate risk points once treatments are complete
2High residual risks formally accepted by executives

Conclusion and key findings

Overall rating
Needs major improvement

Exposure is concentrated in identity, backups and internet-facing systems. Every risk has an owner and a treatment decision: 3 are complete, 17 in progress and 3 not yet started.

R-01

Privileged identities are the single largest exposure

All 38 administrator roles are permanent and 14 rely on push MFA. One phished admin could reach every PHI system. Phishing-resistant MFA and just-in-time elevation reduce the score from 20 to 8.

R-02, R-13

NovaCare cannot yet prove it can recover from ransomware

Backups sit in the production AWS account without immutability, and the last EHR hub restore took 31 hours against a 4-hour objective.

R-04, R-11

Known, exploitable weaknesses are reachable today

A Log4j-vulnerable gateway is internet-facing and 11 live credentials were found in code. Both have containment in place and fixes due by year end.

R-05, R-16

Two High residual risks were accepted, on the record

The EHR partner's qualified SOC 2 and the single-region AWS design remain above appetite. The COO and CTO signed time-bound acceptances with review dates.

Method and criteria

How the assessment was performed

  1. Confirm scope, assets and data flows with system owners.
  2. Identify threat events and the specific vulnerabilities or conditions that enable them.
  3. Record existing controls and rate likelihood (1–5) and impact (1–5) using the criteria table.
  4. Agree a treatment (mitigate, transfer, avoid, accept) and a target residual score with each owner.
  5. Map treatments to NIST SP 800-53 and ISO 27001 Annex A controls; log acceptances above appetite.
  6. Track status monthly and re-score when a treatment is verified.
