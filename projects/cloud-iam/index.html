Cloud Security & Identity Assessment

A technical control assessment of NovaCare's identity platform and AWS environment. Fifteen controls were tested against CIS and CISA SCuBA baselines using configuration exports and logs, with the evidence observed recorded for each.

CIS AWS Foundations v3.0.0CISA SCuBA (Entra ID, M365)NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5Zero Trust (NIST SP 800-207)
OrganizationNovaCare Health Technologies (fictional)
PeriodAug – Sep 2026; status as of Oct 31, 2026
Prepared byEmmanuel Balogun

Engagement summary

Objective

Verify that identity and cloud controls the risk register depends on are configured as intended.

Scope

Entra ID tenant, on-prem AD service accounts, Microsoft 365 sharing, and all 6 AWS accounts.

Approach

Read-only configuration exports, Access Analyzer and sign-in log review; no changes made during testing.

Criteria

CIS AWS Foundations v3.0.0, CISA SCuBA secure configuration baselines, NovaCare access standards.

Conclusion and key findings

Overall rating
Needs major improvement

AWS foundations (root, logging, encryption) are solid. Identity is the weak point: privileged access, lifecycle automation and service accounts all failed.

CIA-01, CIA-02

Every privileged role is permanent

38 standing admin assignments, 9 Global Administrators, and 14 admins on push MFA. Moving to PIM with phishing-resistant MFA addresses R-01.

CIA-08

Wildcard permissions on PHI data stores

Six roles hold rds:* and s3:* on PHI resources; Access Analyzer flags 112 unused permissions.

CIA-06, CIA-07

Identity lifecycle is manual

No HR-driven provisioning, 9 apps outside SSO, and 23 ownerless service accounts, 4 with Domain Admin.

CIA-09, CIA-11, CIA-13

Cloud foundations pass

Organization-wide CloudTrail, hardware MFA on all root users and customer-managed KMS encryption on every PHI store.

Method and criteria

How the assessment was performed

  1. Select controls from CIS and SCuBA baselines that support risks in the register.
  2. Obtain read-only access and export configurations and logs.
  3. Test each control and record the evidence observed, with counts.
  4. Rate Pass, Partial or Fail against the baseline requirement.
  5. Recommend the specific configuration change and owner.
  6. Retest after remediation and update the linked risks.

Result definitions

ResultMeaning
PassMeets the baseline in all accounts or tenants tested
PartialConfigured but with exclusions, gaps in coverage or no monitoring
FailNot configured, or configured in a way that defeats the control
